Merlot Blanc is a white grape variety that is genetically distinct from [[grape.merlot|Merlot]]. Research identified it as an offspring of Merlot and [[grape.folle-blanche|Folle Blanche]], not as a colour mutation of black-berried Merlot. This page still awaits full research and editorial development. [@source.boursiquot-merlot-parentage-2009]
