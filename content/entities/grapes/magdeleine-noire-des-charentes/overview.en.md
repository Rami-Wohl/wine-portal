Magdeleine Noire des Charentes is a rare black grape variety from western France. DNA research identified it as a genetic parent of both [[grape.merlot|Merlot]] and [[grape.malbec|Malbec]]. This page still awaits full research and editorial development. [@source.boursiquot-merlot-parentage-2009]
